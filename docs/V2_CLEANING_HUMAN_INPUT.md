# Bounded General Cleaning human-input tolerance

Advisory revision 3 adds a derived-token normalization layer with original UTF-16
evidence spans. Original title/description and their digests are not rewritten.
It consumes complete fields using bounded request framing, courtesy at sentence
boundaries, contractions in supported frames, and the specific contextual
production `after a party`. `good` does not imply Deep; dwelling context does not
supply areas, scale, or safety facts. Unknown clauses remain material-unclassified
and cannot authorize a cleaning-only subset of a multitask request.

Spelling tolerance is limited to one vowel insertion/deletion/substitution while
preserving all consonants, with a five-character minimum, against `clean`,
`cleaning`, or framing `someone` followed by `to`. The complete result must still
parse. There is no general edit-distance vocabulary, area correction, hazard
rewriting, negation removal or safety inference.

Known missing dimensions use existing unresolved slots. The iOS ViewModel asks
those questions. Material-unclassified prose is described as unaccounted-for work,
not prohibited work. Unchanged text cannot be resubmitted through description
review; the user must actually edit it or cancel.

New explicit reviewed confirmations use cleaning-text-8. Recorded text-2 through
text-7 remain on their original rules, diagnostics and exact-retry results. Scope
review, schedule, required facts, risk confirmations and policy remain mandatory.
There is no new task type or change to the generic dispatcher's supported scope.

A future generic OddJob needs a separately versioned confirmed-job facts and
policy contract. That milestone is explicitly deferred. Unsupported-task and
multitask protections remain in force.

Before E2E, separately authorize selective rollout of the new interpreter and
confirmation bundles plus consumers that revalidate text-8 records. No deployment
or production evidence is implied by local tests.
